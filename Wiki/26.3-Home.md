## 🌟 Overview for Minecraft 26.3

Welcome to the dedicated documentation suite for **Level Does Something v26.3** (compiled for Minecraft `26.3-snapshot-6`).

In vanilla Minecraft, experience levels beyond level 30 serve exclusively as consumable currency for enchanting and anvil repairs. **Level Does Something** introduces an immediate, passive power loop: accumulating and holding raw experience levels grants permanent passive attribute boosts, an ambient celestial particle aura, and audio feedback.
