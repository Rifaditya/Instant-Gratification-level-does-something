## 🌟 Overview for Minecraft 26.3

Welcome to the dedicated documentation suite for **Level Does Something v26.3** (compiled for Minecraft `26.3`).

Accumulating raw experience levels grants permanent passive attribute boosts, an ambient celestial particle aura, and audio feedback.
