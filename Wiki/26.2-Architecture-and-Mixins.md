## ⚡ Performance & Zero-Allocation Invariants

1. **Zero-Allocation Tick Guard**:  
   In `PlayerMixin.tick()`, the attribute handler is only invoked when `lastExperienceLevel != player.experienceLevel`. Normal player walking, sprinting, and combat introduce zero allocation overhead.
2. **Transient Attribute Modifiers**:  
   All modifiers are applied as transient modifiers (`addOrUpdateTransientModifier`), avoiding disk bloat in player `.dat` NBT files.
3. **Client Particle Cull**:  
   `LevelAuraRenderer` immediately skips spectator players (`player.isSpectator()`) and invisible players (`player.isInvisible()`), preventing unnecessary particle computations.
